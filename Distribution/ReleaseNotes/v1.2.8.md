# DropMesh 1.2.8

- Fixes transfers that could appear to do nothing after selecting an online device.
- Keeps incoming transfer authorization synchronized when account devices are added or refreshed.
- Prevents routine account refreshes from interrupting an in-progress incoming connection.
- Improves reconnection reliability and diagnostic reporting without exposing technical details in the user interface.
- Retains automatic pairing for devices signed in to the same Apple account and manual six-digit pairing for other devices.

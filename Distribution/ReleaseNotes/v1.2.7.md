# DropMesh 1.2.7

- Adds Sign in with Apple to the direct Mac build through a secure browser handoff.
- Automatically enrolls devices signed in to the same Apple account into one trusted device group.
- Adds account-based presence and encrypted transfer routing alongside existing manual pairing.
- Adds connection invitations for devices owned by another account.
- Keeps the existing six-digit pairing and local-network transfer paths available.
- Improves reconnect status handling and invitation-link clipboard reliability.

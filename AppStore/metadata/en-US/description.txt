DropMesh lives in your Mac menu bar and helps you send files between paired computers.

Drag files onto the menu bar icon and choose a receiving device to start a transfer. You can also choose files from the menu or send supported clipboard content. Received content is saved in a receiving folder under Downloads, or another folder you authorize.

• Send files and folders, and pair multiple Macs. Choose one recipient for each send.
• Connect over a local network or the internet, with encrypted relay fallback when a direct connection is unavailable.
• View progress and pause, resume or cancel transfers.
• Find received files through notifications, unread indicators and recent arrivals.
• Use Simplified Chinese or English. No DropMesh account registration is needed.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Internet transfers depend on network and service availability. Speed depends on both devices' networks and the route used. Notifications and folder access require system permission.

The Mac App Store edition receives updates through the store. It uses a separate local identity and data from the independently downloaded edition, so switching editions requires pairing again. Do not run both editions at the same time.

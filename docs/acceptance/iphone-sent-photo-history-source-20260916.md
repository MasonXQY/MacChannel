# Sent photo history source — 2026-09-16
Status: implemented and simulator-unit verified; no device install, publish, or identity reset performed.
The Photos picker is explicitly configured with the shared photo library so local asset identifiers are available; identifiers are captured without requesting broad library access and persisted as sent-history source metadata, with no photo/video payload cache retained.
The first Preview/Share resolution requests read/write Photos authorization, accepts full or limited access, fetches the exact identifier, and exports an image or video resource into the action-owned temporary directory with iCloud download enabled.
Denied/restricted access, missing assets/resources, download failure, invalid destinations, and cancellation are explicit; cancellation/failure removes only the exact partial output, while the history action owner releases the directory after use.
English and Simplified Chinese `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` text explains this history-only reread.
Apple basis: `PhotosPickerItem.itemIdentifier` is the local identifier and may be nil without a photo library ([itemIdentifier](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photosui/photospickeritem/itemidentifier)); Photos picker itself needs no broad permission ([picker guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/selecting-photos-and-videos-in-ios)); direct-action authorization and limited access ([privacy guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photokit/delivering-an-enhanced-privacy-experience-in-your-photos-app)); iCloud resource download behavior ([networkAccessAllowed](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/photos/phassetresourcerequestoptions/isnetworkaccessallowed)).

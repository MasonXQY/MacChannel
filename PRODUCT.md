# DropMesh Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

People moving files between their Macs while working. They should be able to pair devices and send by dragging to the menu bar without configuring network infrastructure or creating an account. The currently approved release scope is macOS; Windows is deferred.

## Product Purpose

Provide quick, dependable file, folder and clipboard transfer to a paired device, with automatic receipt into Downloads or a user-selected folder. The App Store edition must preserve compatibility with the existing transfer protocol and respect the independently installed Direct edition.

## Brand Personality

Straightforward, clear, technical. Use short practical language and familiar native macOS interactions. The approved product name is DropMesh.

## Anti-references

Avoid a Telegram-like white paper plane on a deep blue background, as explicitly rejected in the product discussion. Avoid network configuration jargon, invisible menu labels, misleading online states, and long paths to reveal newly received files.

## Design Principles

- Keep the menu bar as the primary entry point and make received files easy to find.
- Ask for permissions when the associated action needs them; explain how to recover when access is denied.
- Keep Settings and Quit reachable during onboarding and service failures.
- Use existing native components and current brand assets; do not introduce a separate visual style for the Store edition.
- Present accurate transfer and availability state; language changes must not interrupt transfers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide the approved Simplified Chinese and English experiences. Preserve native keyboard, focus, accessibility labels, system appearance and reduced-motion behavior. Permission and error states need readable text and an actionable next step rather than color alone.

## Source of Decisions

This records the approved App Store design and implementation plan under `docs/superpowers/`, the existing README workflow, and the product owner's confirmed naming and interaction decisions. It introduces no new product scope or release approval.

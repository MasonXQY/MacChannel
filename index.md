# DropMesh

Private file transfer between your paired Macs, iPhones and iPads. Pair manually, or optionally use Sign in with Apple to connect approved devices and manage invitations. / 在已配对的 Mac、iPhone 和 iPad 之间私密传输文件。你可以手动配对，也可以选择“通过 Apple 登录”来连接获批设备并管理邀请。
